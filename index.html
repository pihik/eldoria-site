What Eldoria does

One map, six kinds of place

Castles, fortresses, chateaux and palaces, manor houses, historic towns and villages, ancient sites. Filter by type, country, condition, UNESCO World Heritage and visited. The map starts with castles and fortresses; add the other kinds in the filters.

A parchment-style map

Zoom out and places group into numbered bubbles. Zoom in and each place gets its own medieval icon, or a dot where it is crowded. In crowded areas the most-read places are drawn first.

Near me

The closest places with distances, and optional alerts while the app is open. Your approximate location stays on the phone.

Read the story

Open a place and read its Wikipedia article inside the app, in any of 14 languages where it exists, including history and legends. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons with the author credited.

Your castle journal

Mark visits with a date, add a star rating, notes and up to three photos. Bookmark places you want to visit. See your visits by country and type. Export a backup file and restore it on a new phone.

Medieval events

A list of upcoming fairs, tournaments and festivals in the countries you choose, with a link to the organiser’s page and to the place in Maps.

Reading the map

Colour shows the kind of place. Shape and size show what is known about its condition.

Large and solid: intact, restored or rebuilt.

Outline only: condition not known.

Small and faded: ruin or destroyed.

Questions and answers

Is Eldoria free?

Yes. There are no ads, no purchases and no account.

Which devices does it run on?

Android. It is being prepared for Google Play; this page will link to it once it is published. There is no iPhone version.

Does it work without internet?

The map, articles and photos need a connection. Your visits, ratings, notes and photos are stored on the phone, and the last downloaded events list is kept for viewing offline.

Where are my visits and reviews stored?

Only on your phone. The developer never receives them. You can export a backup file and restore it on another phone.

Is my location used?

Only if you open Near me and allow it, and only approximately. It is read on the phone while the app is open to sort places by distance, and the app neither stores nor sends it. Details are in the Privacy policy.

Why are so many places “unclassified”?

Public sources state whether a place is intact, restored, rebuilt or a ruin for only about 26% of them. For some of the rest Eldoria infers the condition (for example from the type of site) and marks it unverified; where nothing allows even that, the place stays unclassified.

A place is wrong or missing. What can I do?

In the app, open Settings > Contact, or write to mythicforgeinteractive@gmail.com. The data comes from Wikidata, so corrections made there reach the app with a later data update.

Can I suggest an event?

Yes, through the same Contact form. Events are added by hand from the organisers’ own announcements.
